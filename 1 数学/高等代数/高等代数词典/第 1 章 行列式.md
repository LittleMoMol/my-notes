# 1 行列式
## 1-S1 专题：常见行列式模板
### 1-S1-1 爪形行列式
- **模板 1**：$n$ 阶行列式，其中 $a_i\not=0\ (2\le i \le n)$，则：$|\boldsymbol A| = \begin{vmatrix} a_1 & b_2 & b_3 & \cdots & b_n \\ c_2 & a_2 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\ c_3 & 0 & a_3 & \cdots & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ c_n & 0 & 0 & \cdots & a_n \end{vmatrix} = \left(a_1 - \sum\limits_{i=2}^n \dfrac{b_ic_i}{a_i} \right) \prod\limits_{i=2}^n a_i$ 
	- *计算过程* 
		$|\boldsymbol A| = \begin{vmatrix} a_1 & b_2 & b_3 & \cdots & b_n \\ c_2 & a_2 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\ c_3 & 0 & a_3 & \cdots & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ c_n & 0 & 0 & \cdots & a_n \end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix} a_1 - \sum\limits_{i=2}^n\dfrac{b_ic_i}{a_1} & b_2 & b_3 & \cdots & b_n \\ 0 & a_2 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & a_3 & \cdots & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & a_n \end{vmatrix} = \left(a_1 - \sum\limits_{i=2}^n \dfrac{b_ic_i}{a_i} \right) \prod\limits_{i=2}^n a_i$ 
		注意：如果去掉 $a_i\not=0\ (2\le i \le n)$ 的条件，我们仍可求出 $|\boldsymbol A| = \prod\limits_{i=1}^n a_i - \sum\limits_{i=2}^n \left(b_ic_i\prod\limits_{\substack{j=2\\j\not=i}}^n a_j \right)$ 
- **模板 2**：$n$ 阶行列式，其中 $a_i\not=0\ (1\le i \le n)$，则：$|\boldsymbol A| = \begin{vmatrix} x_1-a_1 & x_2 & x_3 & \cdots & x_n \\ x_1 & x_2-a_2 & x_3 & \cdots & x_n \\ x_1 & x_2 & x_3-a_3 & \cdots & x_n \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ x_1 & x_2 & x_3 & \cdots & x_n-a_n \end{vmatrix} = (-1)^{n-1}\prod\limits_{i=1}^n a_i \left( \sum\limits_{i=1}^n \dfrac{x_i}{a_i} - 1 \right)$ 
	- *计算过程* 
		$\begin{aligned}|\boldsymbol A| &= \begin{vmatrix} x_1-a_1 & x_2 & x_3 & \cdots & x_n \\ x_1 & x_2-a_2 & x_3 & \cdots & x_n \\ x_1 & x_2 & x_3-a_3 & \cdots & x_n \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ x_1 & x_2 & x_3 & \cdots & x_n-a_n \end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix} x_1-a_1 & x_2 & x_3 & \cdots & x_n \\ a_1 & -a_2 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\ a_1 & 0 & -a_3 & \cdots & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ a_1 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & -a_n \end{vmatrix} \\ &= \left( x_1-a_1 + \sum\limits_{i=2}^n \dfrac{a_1x_i}{a_i} \right) (-1)^{n-1} \prod\limits_{i=2}^n a_i = (-1)^{n-1}\prod\limits_{i=1}^n a_i \left( \sum\limits_{i=1}^n \dfrac{x_i}{a_i} - 1 \right)\end{aligned}$ 
		若去掉 $a_i\not=0\ (1\le i \le n)$ 的条件，我们仍可求出 $|\boldsymbol A| = (-1)^{n-1} \sum\limits_{i=1}^n\left( x_i\prod\limits_{\substack{j=1 \\ j\not=i}}^n a_j \right) + (-1)^n\prod\limits_{i=1}^n a_i$ 
	- 这个也是一个有用的模板（除主对角元素外，每行都一样）
### 1-S1-2 三对角行列式
- **模板 1**：$n$ 阶行列式 $D_n = \begin{vmatrix} a_1 & b_1 \\ c_1 & a_2 & b_2 \\ & c_2 & a_3 & \ddots \\ && \ddots & \ddots & \ddots \\ &&& \ddots & a_{n-1} & b_{n-1} \\ &&&& c_{n-1} & a_n \end{vmatrix}$ 的递推关系式为 $D_n = \begin{cases} 1 & n = 0 \\ a_1 & n = 1 \\ a_nD_{n-1} - b_{n-1}c_{n-1}D_{n-2} & n \ge 2 \end{cases}$ 
	- *计算过程* 
		$D_0=1,\ D_1=a_1$ 是显然的。
		$n\ge2$ 时，注意到 $a_n$ 的余子式是 $D_{n-1}$，$b_n$ 的余子式中的 $c_{n-1}$ 的余子式是 $D_{n-2}$，故 $D_n$ 按最后一列进行展开可得：$D_n = a_nD_{n-1} - b_{n-1}c_{n-1}D_{n-2}$ 
- **模板 2**：$n$ 阶行列式 $D_n = \begin{vmatrix} a & b \\ c & a & b \\ & c & a & \ddots \\ && \ddots & \ddots & \ddots \\ &&& \ddots & a & b \\ &&&& c & a \end{vmatrix} = \begin{cases} \dfrac{\alpha^{n+1} - \beta^{n+1}}{\alpha-\beta} & a^2\not= 4bc \\ (n+1)\left(\dfrac a2 \right)^n & a^2=4bc \end{cases}$，其中 $\alpha,\beta$ 是方程 $x^2-ax+bc=0$ 的两根
	- *计算过程* 
		可知 $D_0=1,\ D_1=a,\ D_n = aD_{n-1} - bcD_{n-2}\ (n\ge2)$，为了方便后面计算通项，可由 $D_0=1,D_1=a$ 规定 $D_{-1}=0$ 
		令 $a=\alpha+\beta,\ bc = \alpha\beta$，则 $\alpha,\beta$ 是方程 $x^2-ax+bc=0$ 的两根，则：
		$D_n = (\alpha+\beta)D_{n-1}-\alpha\beta D_{n-2}$，进而可知 $\begin{cases} D_n-\alpha D_{n-1} = \beta(D_{n-1}-\alpha D_{n-2}) \\ D_n-\beta D_{n-1} = \alpha(D_{n-1}-\beta D_{n-2}) \end{cases}$，进而可知 $\begin{cases} D_n - \alpha D_{n-1} = \beta^n \\ D_n - \beta D_{n-1} = \alpha^n \end{cases}$ 
		当 $\alpha \not= \beta$ 即 $a^2\not= 4bc$ 时，根据 Cramer 法则，可知 $D_n = \dfrac{\begin{vmatrix} \beta^n & -\alpha \\ \alpha^n & -\beta \end{vmatrix}}{\begin{vmatrix} 1 & -\alpha \\ 1 & -\beta \end{vmatrix}} = \dfrac{\alpha^{n+1} - \beta^{n+1}}{\alpha - \beta}$ 
		当 $\alpha=\beta$ 即 $a^2=4bc$ 时，$\alpha=\beta=\dfrac a2$，则 $D_n - \dfrac a2 D_{n-1} = \left(\dfrac a2\right)^n$，可得 $D_n = (n+1)\left(\dfrac a2\right)^n$ 
- **重要例子 1**：$n$ 阶行列式 $|\boldsymbol A| = \begin{vmatrix} \cos x & 1 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 2\cos x & 1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 2\cos x & 1 & \cdots & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 1 & 2\cos x & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & 1 & 2\cos x \\   \end{vmatrix} = \cos nx$ 
	- *计算过程* 
		令 $D_n = \begin{vmatrix} 2\cos x & 1 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 2\cos x & 1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 2\cos x & 1 & \cdots & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 1 & 2\cos x & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & 1 & 2\cos x \\   \end{vmatrix}$，则有：
		$\begin{aligned} |\boldsymbol A| =& \begin{vmatrix} \cos x & 1 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 2\cos x & 1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 2\cos x & 1 & \cdots & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 1 & 2\cos x & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & 1 & 2\cos x \end{vmatrix} \\ =& \begin{vmatrix} 2\cos x & 1 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 2\cos x & 1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 2\cos x & 1 & \cdots & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 1 & 2\cos x & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & 1 & 2\cos x \end{vmatrix} \\ &- \begin{vmatrix} \cos x & 1 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 2\cos x & 1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 2\cos x & 1 & \cdots & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 1 & 2\cos x & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & 1 & 2\cos x \end{vmatrix} \\ =& D_n - \cos x D_{n-1} \end{aligned}$ 
		令 $\alpha, \beta$ 是方程 $t^2 - 2\cos x \cdot t + 1 = 0$ 的两根，解得 $\begin{cases} \alpha = \cos x + i\sin x \\ \beta = \cos x - i\sin x \end{cases}$ 
		- 若 $x\not= k\pi\ (k\in \mathbb Z)$，则 $\alpha \not= \beta$，从而有：
			$\begin{aligned} D_n =& \dfrac{\alpha^{n+1}-\beta^{n+1}}{\alpha-\beta} = \dfrac{(\cos x + i\sin x)^{n+1}-(\cos x - i\sin x)^{n+1}}{(\cos x + i\sin x)-(\cos x - i\sin x)} \\ =& \dfrac{(\cos(n+1)x + i\sin(n+1)x) - (\cos(n+1)x - i\sin(n+1)x)}{2i\sin x} \\ =& \dfrac{2i\sin(n+1)x}{2i\sin x} = \dfrac{\sin(n+1)x}{\sin x} \end{aligned}$ 
			进而 $|\boldsymbol A| = D_n - \cos x D_{n-1} = \dfrac{\sin(n+1)x - \cos x\sin nx}{\sin x} = \cos nx$ 
		- 若 $x=k\pi\ (k\in\mathbb Z)$，则 $\alpha=\beta$，从而有：
			$D_n = (n+1)\left(\dfrac {2\cos x}2 \right)^n = (n+1)(\cos x)^n = (n+1)(-1)^{kn}$ 
			进而 $|\boldsymbol A| = D_n - \cos x D_{n-1} = (n+1)(-1)^{kn} - (-1)^kn(-1)^{k(n-1)} = (-1)^{kn} = \cos nx$ 
		综上 $|\boldsymbol A| = \cos nx$ 
### 1-S1-3 Cauchy 行列式
- **模板**：$n$ 阶行列式 $|\boldsymbol A| = \begin{vmatrix} \dfrac{1}{a_1+b_1} & \dfrac{1}{a_1+b_2} & \cdots & \dfrac{1}{a_1+b_n} \\ \dfrac{1}{a_2+b_1} & \dfrac{1}{a_2+b_2} & \cdots & \dfrac{1}{a_2+b_n} \\ \vdots & \vdots &  & \vdots \\ \dfrac{1}{a_n+b_1} & \dfrac{1}{a_n+b_2} & \cdots & \dfrac{1}{a_n+b_n} \end{vmatrix} = \dfrac{\prod\limits_{1\le i<j \le n}(a_j-a_i)(b_j-b_i)}{\prod\limits_{i=1}^n\prod\limits_{j=1}^n (a_i+b_j)}$ 
	- *计算过程* 
		令 $D_n = |\boldsymbol A|$，则有：
		$\begin{aligned} D_n =& \begin{vmatrix} \dfrac{1}{a_1+b_1} & \dfrac{1}{a_1+b_2} & \cdots & \dfrac{1}{a_1+b_n} \\ \dfrac{1}{a_2+b_1} & \dfrac{1}{a_2+b_2} & \cdots & \dfrac{1}{a_2+b_n} \\ \vdots & \vdots &  & \vdots \\ \dfrac{1}{a_n+b_1} & \dfrac{1}{a_n+b_2} & \cdots & \dfrac{1}{a_n+b_n} \end{vmatrix} \\ =& \begin{vmatrix} \dfrac{b_n-b_1}{(a_1+b_1)(a_1+b_n)} & \cdots & \dfrac{b_n-b_{n-1}}{(a_1+b_{n-1})(a_1+b_n)} & \dfrac{1}{a_1+b_n} \\ \vdots & & \vdots & \vdots \\ \dfrac{b_n-b_1}{(a_{n-1}+b_1)(a_{n-1}+b_n)} & \cdots & \dfrac{b_n-b_{n-1}}{(a_{n-1}+b_{n-1})(a_{n-1}+b_n)} & \dfrac{1}{a_{n-1}+b_n} \\ \dfrac{b_n-b_1}{(a_n+b_1)(a_n+b_n)} & \cdots & \dfrac{b_n-b_{n-1}}{(a_n+b_{n-1})(a_n+b_n)} & \dfrac{1}{a_n+b_n} \\  \end{vmatrix} \\ =& \dfrac{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{n-1}(b_n-b_i)}{\prod\limits_{j=1}^n(a_j+b_n)} \cdot \begin{vmatrix} \dfrac{1}{a_1+b_1} & \cdots & \dfrac{1}{a_1+b_{n-1}} & 1 \\ \vdots & & \vdots & \vdots \\ \dfrac{1}{a_{n-1}+b_1} & \cdots & \dfrac{1}{a_{n-1}+b_{n-1}} & 1 \\  \dfrac{1}{a_n+b_1} & \cdots & \dfrac{1}{a_n+b_{n-1}} & 1 \end{vmatrix} \\ =& \dfrac{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{n-1}(b_n-b_i)}{\prod\limits_{j=1}^n(a_j+b_n)} \cdot \begin{vmatrix} \dfrac{a_n-a_1}{(a_1+b_1)(a_n+b_1)} & \cdots & \dfrac{a_n-a_1}{(a_1+b_{n-1})(a_n+b_{n-1})} & 0 \\ \vdots & & \vdots & \vdots \\ \dfrac{a_n-a_{n-1}}{(a_{n-1}+b_1)(a_n+b_1)} & \cdots & \dfrac{a_n-a_{n-1}}{(a_{n-1}+b_{n-1})(a_n+b_{n-1})} & 0 \\ \dfrac{1}{a_n+b_1} & \cdots & \dfrac{1}{a_n+b_{n-1}} & 1 \end{vmatrix} \\ =& \dfrac{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{n-1}(a_n-a_i)(b_n-b_i)}{\prod\limits_{j=1}^n(a_j+b_n)\prod\limits_{k=1}^{n-1}(a_n+b_k)} \cdot \begin{vmatrix} \dfrac{1}{a_1+b_1} & \cdots & \dfrac{1}{a_1+b_{n-1}} \\ \vdots & & \vdots \\ \dfrac{1}{a_{n-1}+b_1} & \cdots & \dfrac{1}{a_{n-1}+b_{n-1}} \end{vmatrix} \\ =& \dfrac{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{n-1}(a_n-a_i)(b_n-b_i)}{\prod\limits_{j=1}^n(a_j+b_n)\prod\limits_{k=1}^{n-1}(a_n+b_k)} \cdot D_{n-1} \end{aligned}$ 
		根据 $\begin{cases} D_n = \dfrac{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{n-1}(a_n-a_i)(b_n-b_i)}{\prod\limits_{j=1}^n(a_j+b_n)\prod\limits_{k=1}^{n-1}(a_n+b_k)} \cdot D_{n-1} \\ D_{n-1} = \dfrac{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{n-2}(a_{n-1}-a_i)(b_{n-1}-b_i)}{\prod\limits_{j=1}^{n-1}(a_j+b_{n-1})\prod\limits_{k=1}^{n-2}(a_{n-1}+b_k)} \cdot D_{n-2} \\ \cdots \\ D_2 = \dfrac{(a_2-a_1)(b_2-b_1)}{(a_1+b_2)(a_2+b_2)\cdot (a_2+b_1)} \cdot D_{1} \\ D_1 = \dfrac{1}{a_1+b_1} \end{cases}$，可知 $D_n = \dfrac{\prod\limits_{1\le i<j \le n}(a_j-a_i)(b_j-b_i)}{\prod\limits_{k=1}^n\left(\prod\limits_{i=1}^k(a_i+b_k)\prod\limits_{j=1}^{k-1} (a_k+b_j)\right)} = \dfrac{\prod\limits_{1\le i<j \le n}(a_j-a_i)(b_j-b_i)}{\prod\limits_{i=1}^n\prod\limits_{j=1}^n (a_i+b_j)}$ 
		即 $|\boldsymbol A| = \dfrac{\prod\limits_{1\le i<j \le n}(a_j-a_i)(b_j-b_i)}{\prod\limits_{i=1}^n\prod\limits_{j=1}^n (a_i+b_j)}$ 
### 1-S1-4 Vandermonde 行列式
- **模板 1**：$n$ 阶行列式 $V_n = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & x_1 & x_1^2 & \cdots & x_1^{n-1} \\ 1 & x_2 & x_2^2 & \cdots & x_2^{n-1} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 1 & x_{n-1} & x_{n-1}^2 & \cdots & x_{n-1}^{n-1} \\ 1 & x_n & x_n^2 & \cdots & x_n^{n-1} \\  \end{vmatrix} = \prod\limits_{1\le i < j \le n} (x_j-x_i)$ 
	- *计算过程* 
		推导如下：$$
		\begin{aligned}
		V_n =& \begin{vmatrix} 1 & x_1 & x_1^2 & \cdots & x_1^{n-1} \\ 1 & x_2 & x_2^2 & \cdots & x_2^{n-1} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 1 & x_{n-1} & x_{n-1}^2 & \cdots & x_{n-1}^{n-1} \\ 1 & x_n & x_n^2 & \cdots & x_n^{n-1} \\  \end{vmatrix} \\
		=& \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\ 1 & x_{2}-x_1 & x_{2}(x_{2}-x_1) & \cdots & x_{2}^{n-2}(x_{2}-x_1) \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 1 & x_{n-1}-x_1 & x_{n-1}(x_{n-1}-x_1) & \cdots & x_{n-1}^{n-2}(x_{n-1}-x_1) \\ 1 & x_{n}-x_1 & x_{n}(x_{n}-x_1) & \cdots & x_{n}^{n-2}(x_n-x_1) \end{vmatrix} \\
		=& \begin{vmatrix} x_{2}-x_1 & x_{2}(x_{2}-x_1) & \cdots & x_{2}^{n-2}(x_{2}-x_1) \\ \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ x_{n-1}-x_1 & x_{n-1}(x_{n-1}-x_1) & \cdots & x_{n-1}^{n-2}(x_{n-1}-x_1) \\ x_{n}-x_1 & x_{n}(x_{n}-x_1) & \cdots & x_{n}^{n-2}(x_n-x_1) \end{vmatrix} \\
		=& \prod\limits_{i=2}^n(x_i-x_1) \begin{vmatrix} 1 & x_2 & \cdots & x_2^{n-2} \\ \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 1 & x_{n-1} & \cdots & x_{n-1}^{n-2} \\ 1 & x_n & \cdots & x_n^{n-2} \\  \end{vmatrix} \\
		=& \cdots \\ 
		=& \left(\prod\limits_{i=2}^n(x_i-x_1)\right)\cdot \left(\prod\limits_{i=3}^n(x_i-x_2)\right)\cdots \left(\prod\limits_{i=n}^n(x_i-x_{n-1})\right) \\
		=& \prod\limits_{1\le i<j \le n} (x_j-x_i)
		\end{aligned}
		$$
- **模板 2**：$n$ 阶行列式 $|\boldsymbol A| = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & x_1 & \cdots & x_1^{i-1} & x_1^{i+1} & \cdots & x_1^n \\ 1 & x_2 & \cdots & x_2^{i-1} & x_2^{i+1} & \cdots & x_2^n \\ \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 1 & x_n & \cdots & x_n^{i-1} & x_n^{i+1} & \cdots & x_n^n \end{vmatrix} = \sum\limits_{1\le k_1<k_2<\cdots<k_{n-i}\le n} \prod\limits_{j=1}^{n-i}x_{k_j} \prod\limits_{1\le i < j \le n} (x_j-x_i)$  
	- *计算过程* 
		注意到这个行列式和 Vandermonde 行列式的区别在于：它缺少 $i$ 次幂的列。
		现添上一行一列使之称为 Vandermonde 行列式，再求出 $y^i$ 的系数即可得到结果为：
		$\begin{aligned} |\boldsymbol B| =& \begin{vmatrix} 1 & x_1 & \cdots & x_1^{i-1} & x_1^i & x_1^{i+1} & \cdots & x_1^n \\ 1 & x_2 & \cdots & x_2^{i-1} & x_2^i & x_2^{i+1} & \cdots & x_2^n \\ \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 1 & x_n & \cdots & x_n^{i-1} & x_n^i & x_n^{i+1} & \cdots & x_n^n \\ 1 & y & \cdots & y^{i-1} & y^i & y^{i+1} & \cdots & y^n \end{vmatrix} \\ =& (y-x_1)(y-x_2)\cdots(y-x_n) \prod\limits_{1\le i < j \le n} (x_j-x_i)\end{aligned}$ 
		因此 $y^i$ 的系数是 $\sum\limits_{1\le k_1 < k_2 < \cdots < k_{n-i} \le n} (-1)^{n-i}x_{k_1}x_{k_2}\cdots x_{k_{n-i}}\prod\limits_{1\le i < j \le n} (x_j-x_i)$ 
		而 $|\boldsymbol B|$ 中元素 $y^i$ 的代数余子式为 $(-1)^{n+1+i+1}|\boldsymbol A| = (-1)^{n+i}|\boldsymbol A|$ 
		因此 $\sum\limits_{1\le k_1 < k_2 < \cdots < k_{n-i} \le n} (-1)^{n-i}x_{k_1}x_{k_2}\cdots x_{k_{n-i}}\prod\limits_{1\le i < j \le n} (x_j-x_i) = (-1)^{n+i}|\boldsymbol A|$ 
		$|\boldsymbol A| = \sum\limits_{1\le k_1 < k_2 < \cdots < k_{n-i} \le n}x_{k_1}x_{k_2}\cdots x_{k_{n-i}}\prod\limits_{1\le i < j \le n} (x_j-x_i)$，即 $|\boldsymbol A| = \sum\limits_{1\le k_1<k_2<\cdots<k_{n-i}\le n} \prod\limits_{j=1}^{n-i}x_{k_j} \prod\limits_{1\le i < j \le n} (x_j-x_i)$
